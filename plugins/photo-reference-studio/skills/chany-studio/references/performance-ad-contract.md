# Conversion creative: one brief, format-specific execution

Use for performance marketing, purchase-oriented ads, Meta reference adaptation, or supplied reference + product/service → advertising. This is a mode of existing owners, not another orchestrator or a compulsory full campaign. Read [thirteen-role adaptation](thirteen-role-adaptation.md) when structuring the argument; read [MoAI chaining](moai-specialist-chain.md) only for a relevant optional contribution.

## Ownership and runtime

- Single image/carousel: `chany-ad-creative`; video: `chany-campaign-video`; multi-format set: `chany-studio`. The requesting owner keeps responsibility after each research handoff.
- Customer decisions that need analysis: `chany-jtbd-persona`; public ad research: `chany-meta-ad-research`; observed account results: `chany-marketing-brief`. Do not confuse these evidence classes.
- Run the Chany core in Claude, ChatGPT Work and Codex without requiring MoAI. Reuse approved upstream work and call a MoAI specialist only when requested or a specific unresolved specialist need warrants it and it is actually callable. No all-skills chain, automatic MoAI installation, or claimed activation from cached files.
- Use natural-language requests in both hosts. Use host-exposed skill selection/invocation, not hardcoded Claude commands in ChatGPT. New skills do not register a portable slash command. Project setup remains optional and owned by `chany-project`; `--with-moai` applies to its project phase, not automatic execution of every specialist.
- Read project/attachments before asking questions. Ask only for missing business decisions; use Claude's question tool or the active ChatGPT/Codex structured-input tool when exposed and allowed, otherwise concise text. Empty responses are not approval. Do not ask beginners for lenses, palettes or copy formulas.
- Check actual browser, preview, image, video and editing capabilities separately. A local machine's installed extension or CLI does not prove access from hosted ChatGPT or Cowork. Keep plan-ready, media-produced, assembled, displayed and published as distinct states. Missing tools permit honest planning/handoff, not fabricated execution.

## Minimal chain

1. Reuse the offer facts, authority assets, customer evidence and desired business result. If unclear, use a compact JTBD analysis; do not force nine jobs, three personas or review collection. One primary job per ad unit.
2. If benchmarking is requested, use Meta for video, single-image and carousel ads. Pinterest is an optional **still photography/style** reference only; never the video reference route. Meta analysis does not require a Pinterest board. Other existing photography/award modes remain separately scoped, not automatic additions to this conversion workflow.
3. Supplied video links/files go through `chany-video-reference-ingest`. Supplied stills, PDFs or storyboards can be inspected with the host's available tools: record actual page/region evidence; audio, cuts and motion remain `not_observed`. An inaccessible reference supports an independent draft only, not a claimed adaptation. Supplied references need no replacement search.
4. Map observed design/hook/copy/proof/CTA mechanisms to our actual product or service and produce one `conversion_brief` in the existing brief/state, not another competing brief. Use the canonical claim ledger from `chany-marketing-brief/references/brief-schema.md`; stable claim IDs link every material promise to its own evidence.
5. Reuse an approved direction. Otherwise use the shared creative-direction selection once for the entire requested set, not once per owner. A supplied reference alone is not an approved concept. No mandatory separately generated campaign master for a single ad: its approved governing layout/still can establish the visual system. Do not create unrequested variants.
6. The output owner adapts the argument to timed video beats, carousel cards or single-image reading hierarchy; thirteen roles are selective, not thirteen required outputs. Approve exact copy and concept before production, then use existing generation, assembly and publication gates without duplicating their job lifecycle.

## Compact conversion brief

Store `conversion_brief` with:

- `version_id`, `offer_version_id`, `insight_version_id` (or an attributed existing customer source), `reference_packet_ids`, `claim_ledger_version_id`, `creative_direction_version_id`, `copy_version_id`;
- product/service/hybrid, customer decision situation, awareness and purchase complexity when known, `primary_job_id`, main purchase barrier, one promise and actual proof;
- `business_goal`, `conversion_event`, `primary_metric`, `diagnostic_metrics`, `guardrail_metrics`, destination, actual offer/terms and limitations;
- requested `ad_units[]` with stable ID, format, placement, count, carousel card count when relevant, duration/ratio, language, owner and acceptance criteria;
- the selected mechanism → our original adaptation map, selective role coverage, evidence gaps, pending approvals and `draft | approved | invalidated` status.

For purchase goals, judge purchase/CPA/ROAS when data supports them, not views or CTR alone. For services without checkout, distinguish qualified inquiry, booking and eventual sale; do not silently equate a submitted form to revenue. No universal target ROAS, budget multiple, expected uplift or sample size. Missing measurement data does not prevent a clearly labeled production hypothesis.

## Copy and design compiler

Translate supported feature → practical benefit → relevant felt outcome, checking evidence at every step; a mechanism never proves an outcome by itself. Choose only the persuasion device needed (e.g. demonstration, problem/solution, comparison or explanation), not stacked formulas. Avoid manufactured fear, fake testimonials, sensitive-trait insinuation and invented urgency.

Compile production prompts with the shared creative-direction sections plus `ad_unit_id`, `job_id`, `message_job`, `claim_refs`, `copy_version_id` and format-specific scene/card ID. Art direction must specify focal hierarchy, subject scale, crop, palette/contrast, material/light, type roles, safe copy area and brand device. A trend is optional and must aid the purchase argument and readability, not merely look fashionable.

Maintain an `ad_copy_packet`: exact `in_creative_copy`, `primary_text`, `platform_headline`, `platform_description`, `platform_cta`, `destination_url`, required qualifications, `claim_refs` and approval/version. Include card-level copy where applicable. Fields unsupported by the current placement are `not_applicable`; unknown source fields are `not_observed`, not invented. Validate current limits and supported CTA labels through the platform adapter. Separate image plates from exact typesetting when needed.

For Korean copy, remove abstract clichés and translation-like syntax while preserving every approved fact anchor. Optional MoAI copy/humanize suggestions are drafts; any accepted wording change creates a new copy version and reopens affected layout/claim review. Never “humanize” numbers, offers or mandatory qualifiers away.

## Message match and learning

Compare the ad promise, actual offer/terms, CTA and user's destination first screen/action. Semantic agreement matters; identical wording is not mandatory. Mark a missing/inaccessible destination `unverified`. Do not browse competitor destinations, edit the user's website, configure pixels or change accounts as an implicit next step. Do not diagnose “low CVR = bad landing” without considering traffic quality, tracking, attribution, offer and checkout evidence.

Deliver the requested media, accompanying ad copy, card/scene order, source/adaptation note, destination-check status and one test hypothesis when useful. Reference count, ad-unit count, carousel card count and billable job count are different quantities. Show actual preview status; links or a contact sheet alone do not prove all production files exist.

If results are supplied, let `chany-marketing-brief` review linked versions, time windows, metric definitions, attribution, sample and comparable delivery before a conclusion. Define one variable, fixed properties, primary metric, diagnostics, guardrails and stop/decision rule. Simple side-by-side delivery is not automatically a randomized A/B test. Return inconclusive/invalid when appropriate; do not create or launch variants without scope approval.

Changing the offer, insight, selected reference, copy or creative direction invalidates dependent plans/assets/reviews only; preserve unaffected accepted siblings. Store version references in the existing campaign state. Analysis and planning never authorize paid generation, external uploads, account writes, publication or budget/activation changes.
