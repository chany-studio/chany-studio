# Video-first experience

Video is a primary finished product, not an upsell after making photos. Apply this contract when the user requests a video. Explicit still-image, analysis-only, prompt-only, drama and editable Adobe requests retain their existing owners and scope. This contract coordinates existing capabilities; it does not claim a new video engine or installed UI.

## One short intake

Inspect the user's product/service evidence and any reference link or file first. Reuse known purpose, placement, duration, ratio and copy. Ask one unresolved question at a time, with plain-language choices, under work-first-interview.md. Do not require a camera/model vocabulary or project setup.

- If the video purpose is missing and materially changes the film, offer “제품·서비스 소개 / 사용 방법 보여주기 / 광고·문의 유도”. These are choices, not an assumption that every film is a conversion ad.
- Existing reference → inspect it; “찾아줘” → find video references; “없이” → propose an original direction. A product photo remains identity evidence and never implies that the final output should be a photo.
- Recommend a duration/ratio and sound plan from the intended use when those are unknown. Label them as recommendations in the compact production plan instead of adding a separate settings questionnaire. Confirm them within the applicable production card before spending. Do not prescribe the same duration or hook to every film.

## Video reference discovery

For an ordinary product/service film, the output owner remains chany-campaign-video. A previously selected narrative, Adobe, remake or other video specialist remains the owner and delegates only missing reference inspection. Conversion advertising uses chany-meta-ad-research; named creators, brand films, service demos and curated public examples use the existing chany-reference-board curated-source lane, explicitly scoped to video, followed by chany-video-reference-ingest. Do not invoke that owner's generic Pinterest image-board default. The static-source allowlists still apply to static lanes.

For curated film/motion discovery on Vimeo Staff Picks or Motionographer with an unspecified count, propose three relevant video candidates when accessible, labelled with source, why it suits the user's product/service, observable opening/action/ending and what was actually viewed. Meta ad research and Ads of the World case research keep their specialist six-candidate defaults, including video ads; an explicit count overrides either default. A thumbnail, search snippet or static screenshot cannot establish movement, timing, voice or music. Show a playable preview only when the host supports it; otherwise use the source link and clearly mark the observation limit. Missing access means a partial discovery result, never invented viewing or performance claims. Metricool is optional account evidence, not a required video search engine.

A supplied short reference plus product uses chany-viral-product-remake; service films remain with chany-campaign-video. Ingest clips/links before using timing claims. For authorized source editing, use the bounded video-edit branch; trim/caption/timeline changes use chany-video-assembly. Public reference links alone do not authorize downloading, third-party upload, replacement or republication. Retain the existing original-remake versus authorized-source-edit distinction.

## Plan through final delivery

Show one compact plan: intended finished video, duration/ratio, opening → demonstration/evidence → ending, protected product/service facts, exact copy, and voice/music/effects/captions as requested. Story and pacing belong to the video owner; a static layout is not a motion plan. Separate product movement from camera movement and specify transitions/continuity only where useful.

Reuse accepted stills and source footage. Create governing stills only when the existing scene/identity contract requires them; do not create an unrelated photo campaign or sell extra images as a prerequisite. Retain the required still and video paid checkpoints when both stages are needed. Source-only editing or an already accepted first frame must not trigger a redundant still generation. Live schemas, input limits, costs and submitted-job recovery remain with the media production loop. Do not infer that one final video equals one paid job or promise a total that excludes required paid stages.

For a requested finished edit: approved scene plan → accepted generated/supplied clip versions → assembly → requested captions/sound → inspect the final export. Keep reference/brand/copy decisions across every handoff. A generation provider's success status, storyboard, thumbnail, or set of clips does not establish completed delivery. If a capability is unavailable, name the missing stage and return the completed work with an honest remaining-work status.

## Playback and easy revisions

Display the actual playable export or usable output file before the short review. Check at the available evidence level:

- Product/service identity throughout motion: shape, labels, UI truth and count; include interaction moments and last frame.
- Motion and edit: physical plausibility, flicker, continuity, transitions, opening/ending and actual total duration.
- Captions and sound: exact text, readable duration, crop-safe placement; presence, timing, intelligibility and authorized assets when sound is requested. Do not mark audio passed from screenshots or metadata alone. Requested silence is valid.

Offer up to three relevant changes: “첫 장면·속도 바꾸기”, “제품이 달라진 장면 고치기”, “자막·목소리·음악 수정”. Record the affected timestamp/shot when observable; preserve accepted clips and audio. Use deterministic editing for timing/captions where supported; regenerate only the affected scene when generative repair is necessary and approved. Preserve cost/retry limits. Report final format, duration, included sound/captions and any unchecked item. No automatic upload or publication follows delivery.
