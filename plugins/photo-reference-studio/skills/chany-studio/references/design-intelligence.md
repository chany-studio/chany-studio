# Design craft without trend scouting

Use for carousel, thumbnail and static advertising work. This is an internal design guide, not a website-trend search workflow. Do not automatically visit Midjourney, Reddit, X or Threads for trends. Existing requested reference discovery (including Pinterest/MeiGen) and official capability/specification verification remain separate.

A user-supplied reference or accepted campaign style takes priority. Inspect the image, separate style from product authority, and apply reference-led-design.md. Users do not need vocabulary or prompts; ask only about unresolved visible differences.

## Design decisions

Select a coherent treatment suited to the audience, message and supplied reference. Record internally only what the job needs: hierarchy/grid, type scale, palette/contrast, crop, light/material treatment and copy space. Product truth and small-screen readability outrank decorative effects.

- Editorial restraint: a meaningful hero image, purposeful negative space and restrained hierarchy; not tiny required text.
- Tactile realism: credible textures/reflections and context without altering packaging or implying unsupported benefits.
- Typographic contrast: a short clear hook with quieter support, never hiding the subject or label.
- Modular sequencing: consistent margins, typography and palette with varied image/text rhythm, not repeated posters.

These are craft choices, not claims of current trends or proven conversion. Do not equate premium design with beige palettes, excessive whitespace or generic effects.

## Produce and inspect

Use chany-ad-creative/references/social-design-formats.md for the requested format. Compile input roles + chosen treatment + composition + copy space + exact copy + output constraints internally. Use separate typesetting when exact copy requires it.

Review the actual final result at mobile size: recognizable subject, deliberate reading order, Korean legibility, alignment/spacing, brand continuity and truthful hook. Compare to the reference's intended appearance separately from product fidelity. For multiple cards, check individual readability and whole-set rhythm. Correct the highest-impact observed defect within the existing quality-loop budget; do not regenerate accepted siblings.

Show finished files/previews first. A prompt, plan or provider success receipt is not a completed designed asset. Keep technical prompts available on request, not as homework.
