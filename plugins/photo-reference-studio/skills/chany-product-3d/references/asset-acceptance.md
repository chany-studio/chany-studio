# Product 3D acceptance

Record `observed`, `reconstructed`, `must match`, and `result` for silhouette, proportions, distinctive geometry, count, label orientation, logo/text, material, transparency, seams, underside/back, scale, pivot, normals, UV/texture seams, topology, and each approved camera view.

An unseen or unreadable property cannot be marked matched. Treat a 3D asset as advertising reconstruction unless verified measurements and engineering sources establish otherwise.
