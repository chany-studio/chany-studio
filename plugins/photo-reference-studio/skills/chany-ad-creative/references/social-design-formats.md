# Social design modes

Keep one static production owner and load shared design-intelligence and plain-language-direction. A supplied reference replaces discovery unless alternatives were requested.

## Editorial / magazine carousel

Infer topic, audience and takeaway; ask only if missing. Honor the requested count; otherwise propose the smallest useful sequence. Reference-board count is unrelated. Use a flexible sequence: cover promise → useful context → distinct insights/examples → recap/next step. This is not a compulsory sales funnel. Close with save/share/read-more or no explicit CTA as appropriate, not forced "buy now".

Keep grid, margins, typography and palette consistent. Vary full-image, concise text and detail layouts to create rhythm, one dominant thought per card. Do not shrink an essay into small text. Recompose incompatible source ratios. Reuse carousel-ad-contract's manifest, individual assets, ordered preview and per-card checks; omit paid-ad fields when not applicable. Deliver separate ordered cards and requested caption, not just a flattened collage.

## Conversion carousel / static ad

Use existing performance-ad and carousel-ad contracts for proof, hooks, copy and CTA. Meta benchmarks inform the buying argument; visual/prompt sources inform execution. A trend cannot replace evidence. State a testable design hypothesis such as clearer product scale or headline contrast, never unmeasured conversion uplift.

## YouTube thumbnail / Instagram cover

Accurately promise the actual content and remain recognizable small. Use the supplied topic/title and real subject; ask one plain question if absent, never invent content or reactions. Choose one focal subject, foreground/background separation and a short complementary hook when useful; do not repeat a long title or force a purchase CTA. Avoid unsupported before/after outcomes or unrelated clickbait.

Resolve current destination specifications through the publication adapter. YouTube wide thumbnails and Instagram Reel/profile/feed crops are different outputs; protect face/product/headline in the actual crop, not a presumed universal square. Preview at phone/feed size for readability, visual hierarchy and UI overlap. Preserve supplied faces/products and exact wording. Deliver requested cover files, not a video or unrequested variants.

## Easy requests

- "이 제품과 첨부한 잡지 느낌으로 인스타 캐러셀 5장 만들어줘. 전문적인 건 알아서 추천해줘."
- "작은 카페 조명 바꾸는 영상이야. 이 사진으로 유튜브 썸네일을 깔끔하게 만들어줘."
- "메타 광고를 만들어줘. 첨부 사진의 분위기를 살리되 내 브랜드 색과 제품은 유지해줘."
