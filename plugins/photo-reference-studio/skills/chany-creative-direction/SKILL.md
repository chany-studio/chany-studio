# Chany Creative Direction

Give beginners an expert art director's decision layer without asking them to speak in design jargon.

Read the shared [commercial creative direction system](../chany-studio/references/creative-direction-system.md) and [current creative signals](../chany-studio/references/current-creative-signals.md). Consume the approved industry direction and marketing brief when available.

## Workflow

1. Lock the offer, audience decision, one primary message, evidence, channel, authoritative assets, approved copy, brand constants, and prohibited claims. Separate known facts from creative proposals.
2. If the brief has no meaningful direction, create `brand-core`, `emotional-editorial`, and `trend-forward` territories using the shared scoring model. Make each differ in idea, signature device, composition, light, palette, material, typography and motion—not just colour.
3. Use at most one dominant current Pinterest trend signal and one optional accent. Record why each fits and what is deliberately not borrowed. A trend may be rejected.
4. Recommend the highest-scoring territory and explain the decision in plain language. Do not ask the user to choose lenses, art movements, fonts, colour theory, or technical prompting syntax.
5. Compile the approved or recommended direction into the shared `creative_direction` packet and one production prompt per named output. Adapt composition and safe areas per channel while preserving the same campaign device.
6. Run the anti-genericity gate. Replace vague aesthetic adjectives with observable craft decisions. Keep exact copy, evidence, identity and rights gates intact.

## Reference boundary

This skill does not browse for references. When real references are needed, hand the approved industry taxonomy route to exactly one owner: `chany-reference-board` for broad Pinterest discovery, `chany-commercial-photo-reference` for Production Paradise commercial photography, or `chany-award-ad-reference` for approved award and campaign archives. Each owner keeps its own hard source boundary; never blend providers to fill a weak board. Do not use a knowledge-source example image as a reference.

## Output

- three concise concept cards and scores when direction is not already approved
- one recommended concept with a plain-language rationale
- one complete `creative_direction` packet
- one compiled production prompt for each requested content family
- named anti-genericity risks, acceptance criteria, and any unresolved fact or approval

Stop before media generation, paid submission, publication, or live platform action. Production belongs to the relevant Chany owner and the shared media-production loop.
