# Award Advertising Source Policy

## Hard scope

This lane permits only these public award and campaign archives:

| Provider | Accepted source pages | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Ads of the World | `adsoftheworld.com` | contemporary and historical advertising campaigns and curated collections |
| D&AD | `dandad.org` | awarded commercial photography, craft, design, and campaign work |
| The One Show | `oneclub.org` | awarded advertising and design cases |

Search or open only the approved domains. Keep the archive work or case page as provenance and do not follow an external entrant, agency, production, brand, social, store, or download destination. A preview is acceptable only when discovery pairs it to the exact approved page and the host can display actual image content without bypassing access controls.

Stocksy, ShotDeck, Death to Stock, unapproved award mirrors, inspiration aggregators, and stock libraries are explicitly excluded. The Pinterest-only preview MCP connection is not a transport for these sources.

## Query and count contract

Use one exact L1 subject label from the central industry taxonomy and zero or one direct L2 child. Apply the same semantic phrase across the approved archives instead of adding source-specific adjectives. Never add award, winner, best, creative, luxury, cinematic, trendy, platform, mood, color, lighting, camera, agency, brand, or campaign modifiers.

Resolve `target_count` from the user's explicit positive whole number or default to `6`. Deduplicate the same campaign across archives and alternate crops or formats of one execution. For three or more results, use at least two archives when the qualifying pool permits; source spread never justifies a weak or untraceable candidate.

## Acceptance and presentation

Reject inaccessible or login-gated pages, uncertain image-to-case pairing, case pages without a legible hero visual, text-only articles, video-only entries without a still, collages that prevent shot analysis, and results that cannot be rendered as image content in the current conversation.

For each accepted reference retain:

    provider: "Ads of the World | D&AD | The One Show"
    source_url: ""
    search_query: ""
    work_title: ""
    advertiser_or_entrant: ""
    fit_note: ""
    visual_dna: ""
    message_mechanism: ""
    exclude_from_reference: []
    display_confirmed: false
    rights_status: "direction-only"

Separate what transfers—composition, hierarchy, proof device, visual metaphor, sequencing, and attention mechanic—from what does not—logo, slogan, copy, branded characters, packaging, people, proprietary art, and distinctive campaign execution. If the approved pool is exhausted before the target is visible, report `requested`, `visible`, and `shortfall` without widening scope.
