## Adaptation recipes

**Vashi editorial organization:** preserve the idea of an organized selects sequence and a separate main edit. For a short explainer, keep only useful track roles; do not import a feature film's complexity or change the chosen sequence format blindly. The inspected bins are sequences, raw media, photos, graphics, SFX, VFX, titles, music, AE projects, and exports.

**Jarle camera motion:** choose the matching-size or oversized-footage family based on measured source/sequence size. Apply on an expendable duplicate shot. Inspect all four edges throughout the clip and ensure the desired subject remains framed. Do not apply to exact diagrams, readable maps, or a required locked-off opening. The creator's historical performance claims are not a benchmark of this machine.

**Ordinary Folk rig study:** identify controller → reference layer → followers, units of delay, coordinate conversion, and dependence on layer order. Describe the general principle in our own implementation notes; preserve restrictions on the original code and artwork. Reimplement only a generic mechanism independently when needed, with our own assets and a simple test. Do not claim that removing a credit or translating code clears a license.

**Jake/Ben acquisition queue:** keep official landing links and the exact sought resource. If acquisition asks for identity, subscription, or purchase, complete independent work first and obtain the specific missing authorization only if acquisition is necessary. Do not bypass the gate, invent an email, or claim a link is an acquired file.
