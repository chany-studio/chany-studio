# Research and reuse

Checked 2026-11-07. Recheck version-sensitive claims when used. This is an operational synthesis, not a claim to have watched every linked video or mastered every craft.

## Admission criteria

Use four signals: attributable creator/production role; a complete work or before/after; enough source or explanation to reproduce the relevant behavior; applicability to the requested film. Record exactly which signals were observed. A showreel can establish a visual reference, but cannot establish how it was made. A commercial course page can establish availability, but cannot establish knowledge of the paid lessons.

Classify each technique as:

- **Documented:** official behavior or an attributable practitioner's published method.
- **Source inspected:** downloaded project/preset or readable tutorial examined; native execution not yet established.
- **Locally tested:** actual app, version, inputs, operation, and rendered evidence recorded.
- **Production accepted:** reviewed in the intended edit and approved under the current project's process.

A downloaded tutorial with permission to learn is not automatically a commercially reusable template. Retain attribution and the original terms. Do not redistribute restricted source inside a generic skill package. Access failures and account gates are recorded as unavailable, not bypassed.

## What was not admitted as a rule

“Never use Dynamic Link”, “everything should bounce”, “always 60 fps”, “add cinematic LUT”, and “export MP4 for all intermediates” lack a universal basis. Choose by shot, sources, color pipeline, performance, and delivery. Forum praise alone, reposted tutorials, anonymous bulk expression collections, course marketing, and unread video links do not count as learned implementation.

## Add a new source

Record URL, creator, original/repost status, publication and access date if available, evidence inspected, technique, application/version, assets/terms, adaptation, test result, and limitations. A useful reference changes a production decision or supplies a reusable artifact. Keep the library selective rather than collecting every available download.
